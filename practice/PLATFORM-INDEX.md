# Platform and Source Index

Indeks ini mencatat sumber yang pernah dikumpulkan untuk jalur belajar. **Daftar ini bukan bukti bahwa seluruh challenge boleh dipublikasikan, gratis, aktif, atau masih tersedia.** Periksa aturan, akses, biaya, dan izin pada sumber resmi sebelum mengerjakan atau mempublikasikan materi.

Folder write-up hanya dibuat ketika sudah ada challenge yang selesai dan publikasinya diizinkan. Gunakan nama platform sebagai folder; wilayah di bawah ini adalah pengelompokan katalog dari daftar yang dikumpulkan, bukan verifikasi lokasi hukum/organisasi setiap penyedia.

## Sources that are not ordinary practice-write-up platforms

- `CTFtime` — event discovery/calendar and event information aggregator. Use official event pages/rules as the source of publication permission; a listing on CTFtime is not permission to publish solutions.
- `HackerOne`, `Bugcrowd`, `Immunefi`, `Internet Bug Bounty` — vulnerability disclosure/bug bounty ecosystems. Reports belong in an authorized research/disclosure workflow, and must follow scope, embargo, disclosure, and program terms.
- `Google Project Zero` — security research publication/resource, not a generic challenge platform.
- `Google KernelCTF` — vulnerability-research programme; follow its current scope, reporting, and disclosure rules rather than treating it as an unrestricted practice platform.
- `Shellphish how2heap` — heap-exploitation reference/tutorial code, not an ordinary CTF platform.

These entries are retained as learning/research references, not as a statement that every one belongs in `practice/`. For real vulnerability research, use `reverse-engineering-lab/Research/` only when the disclosure status permits public release.

## Publication status tracking

For every public write-up, record at least:

- Official challenge/source URL.
- Activity type: self-paced practice or official competition.
- Official rules or publication-policy URL.
- Date the rules were checked.
- Whether a full solution may be published.
- Whether flags, screenshots, binaries, source files, and attachments may be redistributed.
- Any embargo, review, or disclosure conditions.

When a permission status is unclear, do not publish the solution or third-party files.
